Red Peaks (RNA-Seq Data): The red peaks correspond to gene expression, with a strong peak indicating significant transcriptional activity. This suggests a gene in this region is actively being transcribed and could be highly expressed.

Blue Peaks (ATAC-Seq Data): The blue peaks highlight regions of open chromatin, likely non-coding OCRs, which are associated with regulatory elements rather than protein-coding regions. These open chromatin areas suggest active regulatory regions that may influence nearby gene expression.

Green Boxes (Gene Annotations): The green boxes delineate gene structures, marking exons. The alignment of the non-coding open chromatin regions with these annotations indicates a potential role in gene regulation, further supporting the idea of active regulation in this region.
